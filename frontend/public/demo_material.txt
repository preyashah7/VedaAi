Sample Reading Passage for Demo

Title: The Hidden Stream

Once, in a small village nestled between two low hills, there was a stream the villagers forgot to name. Children played at its banks, fishermen mended nets, and elders would sit and watch the sunlight scatter over the water as if it were a handful of coins. The stream moved quietly, carrying leaves and stories toward places no one quite remembered.

On Monday mornings, the market over by the eastern road filled with voices and spices. On afternoons, students from the village school would bring borrowed books and sit beneath the sycamore tree, reading aloud to anyone who would listen. The stream served as a gentle companion to all these moments; it hummed like a soft instrument beneath the louder noises of the day.

Sometimes, people found small things in the stream—an ornate button, a note on faded paper, a pressed flower. Each found object seemed to be a piece of some larger, invisible puzzle. No one could say where the objects had come from, only that the stream had returned them as if keeping a careful ledger.

Short Questions (for demo):
1. Where was the village located?
2. What did villagers do by the stream?
3. Describe one object people found in the stream.

Writing Prompt:
Imagine you follow the stream downstream to discover its source. Write a short paragraph (4–6 sentences) describing what you find and why it matters to the village.

Sample Vocabulary List:
- Nestled
- Ornate
- Ledger
- Companion

End of demo material.
